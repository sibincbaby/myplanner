## Viable Projects

It is novel in the narrow sense that matters: seven prior-art phrasings across two rounds turned up no OSS project that enforces `AskUserQuestion` as the way a working turn ends. Daily utility is the strong leg — every turn of every session ends, and the closing paragraph you have to re-read and reply to in prose is a tax paid dozens of times a day. Fills-gap is the leg that fails: the card widget itself is first-party (`AskUserQuestion`), and a one-line CLAUDE.md convention gets most of the way there without code, which is why this lands at 3/4 rather than 4/4.
