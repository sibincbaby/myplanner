## 1. Most interesting discoveries

### TECHO5: one open Linux firmware for every unlockable Echo (latest tag v1.6.49; pushed 2026-11-23)
- **What it is:** [HuskerMinion/techo5](https://github.com/HuskerMinion/techo5) (810★, created 2026-09-14, MIT) replaces Fire OS and Alexa with "a small Alpine Linux image and one Go daemon". The result is a local Home Assistant voice satellite with its own touch screen, an on-device wake word, SSH, and signed A/B over-the-air updates that roll back on their own.
- **Devices (README table, statuses as the author reports them):**
  - Echo Show 5 1st gen (`checkers`) and 2nd gen (`cronos`), and Echo Show 8 1st gen (`crown`): "in daily use".
  - **Echo Show 8 2nd gen (`athena`, MediaTek MT8183, 2 GB/8 GB):** runs on the author's own 64-bit **mainline Linux 6.18** kernel, "in daily use on two units" ([techo5-athena](https://github.com/HuskerMinion/techo5-athena), created 2026-10-15).
  - Echo Spot 1st gen ([techo5-spot](https://github.com/HuskerMinion/techo5-spot), 54★) and Echo Dot 2nd gen ([techo5-dot](https://github.com/HuskerMinion/techo5-dot), 300★): "in daily use".
  - Echo 2nd gen and Echo Input: one unit each. Echo Dot 3rd gen: two units.
  - Skylight Calendar 15 (150-CAL): one unit, the newest port. It has no microphone, so it works only as a screen and speaker.
  - Fire HD 8 (10th gen), Galaxy Tab S6 Lite and Meta Portal (2nd gen): through an app, [techo5-panel](https://github.com/HuskerMinion/techo5-panel), with no unlock.
- **How the devices are opened:**
  - The BootROM/preloader exploit [R0rt1z2/amonet](https://github.com/R0rt1z2/amonet) (199★, pushed 2026-09-11) opens the MT8163 Echos.
  - "saiyaku-athena" opens the Show 8 2nd gen. That unlock is credited to R0rt1z2 and bengris32, with a thread on XDA (blocked here).
  - The README states plainly: "**No Echo made after 2021 has a public unlock.**"
- **Mainline kernels:** a separate project, [nuno407/linux-amazon-echo](https://github.com/nuno407/linux-amazon-echo) (created 2026-10-29, pushed 2026-11-17), builds kernel.org **Linux 7.2.13** for `athena` and `cronos`. It notes that a stock unit "only starts images Amazon signed".
- **Conflicting coverage:** a [Hackaday post from 2026-01-02](https://hackaday.com/2026/01/02/jailbreaking-the-amazon-echo-show) (search summary; the page was blocked) said the Show 8 2nd gen was *not* supported by the original exploit. The athena unlock is newer than that coverage, so this is a material change from what most guides still say.
- **Score:** 9/10 · **Confidence:** high for Show 5 and first-gen Show 8 (many users and guides); medium for athena and Skylight (author-reported, one or two units).

### CleanYourTV: Android TV debloating, driven by AI coding agents, goes viral (created 2026-10-17; v3.6.0; ~60k★)
- **What it is:** [Gathin23/cleanyourtv](https://github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv) (60,390★, 7,931 forks today) is a prompt plus scripts for Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI or other coding agents.
  - Over Wi-Fi ADB, the agent backs up the TV's state, disables vendor telemetry, ad and content-row packages with `pm disable-user`, and installs a small native launcher.
  - No root is needed and nothing is uninstalled. A restore script undoes every step.
- **Scale (README "The story" section, the author's own numbers):** the launch post went up on 2026-10-17. The repo was the #1 trending repository on GitHub for the week of 19–25 October. By 20 November it had 59K stars, and its lists covered **508 TV models from 139 brands**.
- **Limits (README):**
  - Fire TV gets the debloat half only, because "Fire OS will not hand Home to a third-party launcher".
  - On the 2015 Fire TV Box even that fails.
  - Samsung Tizen, LG webOS, Roku, Vizio and Hisense VIDAA are not supported, because they have no ADB.
  - The README also warns about look-alike domains and apps that the project does not run ([#1004](https://github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv/issues/1004)).
  - One secondary summary claims early testing leaned on emulators ([bestforandroid.com](https://bestforandroid.com/radar/people-using-ai-remove-ads-bloatware-from-android-tv.md), search summary only). The 200+ contributor device reports in the repo suggest broad real-device use, but this was not checked unit by unit.
- **Spin-offs, all created in the last five weeks:**
  - [man15h/tellyscope](https://github.com/man15h/tellyscope) (2,208★, created 2026-10-22): a phone app with no PC and no root. It uses 330+ lists adapted from CleanYourTV, has a protected-package list, and gives every change an undo.
  - [HoangTran0410/tv-remote](https://github.com/HoangTran0410/tv-remote) (109★).
  - [Elaine-Lynn/GenTV](https://github.com/Elaine-Lynn/GenTV) (82★).
  - [JohnWayneeee/cleantv](https://github.com/JohnWayneeee/cleantv) (53★): a Claude Code skill that also calibrates picture and sound. It reports free RAM on a 2.8 GB CHiQ TV going from 1.42 GB to 1.77 GB.
- **Why it matters:** this is the clearest case yet of an AI agent acting as the *installer*. The value is a curated, community-maintained package list plus guard rails, and the agent supplies the device-specific judgment.
- **Score:** 8/10 (software freedom is limited: no root, and the vendor's OS stays) · **Confidence:** high

### Taking over Android photo frames: an agent-ready field manual (created 2026-10-08; pushed 2026-11-16)
- **What it is:** [wesbos/photo-frame-dashboard](https://github.com/wesbos/photo-frame-dashboard) (1,828★). It is a set of per-board notes and scripts for getting ADB, full backups, root, a replacement launcher and a kiosk dashboard onto locked-down Android frames.
  - TL;DR: "buy a $50 frame, point an agent at this repo and let 'er rip!"
  - "More than 70 people" have shared builds, including Linux (Omarchy) on an A33 frame.
- **Boards covered (README):**
  - **PF1007L/AiMOR:** Allwinner A33, sold under many 10.1" brand names.
  - **BIUFRAME/Synergy 10X1:** Rockchip RK3126.
  - **Skylight Calendar 150-CAL:** Rockchip RK3566. A `D156.V0.10` userdebug firmware ships with ADB and `su` on, `flash.locked=0` and SELinux permissive ([skylight-d156.md](https://github.com/wesbos/photo-frame-dashboard/blob/main/docs/skylight-d156.md), tested 2026-10-13).
  - **LOOFII C-3 21.5" calendar:** Allwinner A133, root ADB out of the box.
  - **Dromart MI86:** rk312x, rooted from an SD card.
- **Security finding:** [aimor-remote-root-shell.md](https://github.com/wesbos/photo-frame-dashboard/blob/main/docs/aimor-remote-root-shell.md) documents the following on firmware `…PF1007L-321-OS-AIMOR.0.20260113`:
  - The stock launcher app `com.efercro.os.aimor` keeps an **unencrypted** TCP connection to a vendor server.
  - It passes the text of a command type it receives (12009) to setuid `su`.
  - So "the vendor server can run any command as root on the frame", and so can anyone who can tamper with that traffic.
  - This is one project's analysis, with no CVE. The repo explicitly asks readers not to invent one.
- **Related:** [ahilles107/frameo-114k-root](https://github.com/ahilles107/frameo-114k-root) (created 2026-10-12) roots a Frameo 114K (Allwinner A33, Android 6.0.1, FCC ID 2ATFT-102K) entirely from an SD card. The A33 boots the card before its eMMC. Only the 16 MiB boot partition is patched, which gives authenticated root ADB over Wi-Fi. It worked on one unit.
- **Score:** 8/10 · **Confidence:** high for the method (many reports per firmware string); per-unit results vary, and the README stresses that frames under one listing ship different boards.

### Xiaomi TV Box S 2nd Gen runs Ubuntu 26.10 with a *locked* bootloader (created 2026-11-02)
- **What it is:** [jhonnrodr/xiaomibox-ubuntu](https://github.com/jhonnrodr/xiaomibox-ubuntu) (1★, last push 2026-11-03). Model MDZ-28-AA, codename `jaws`: Amlogic S905X4, 2 GB/8 GB, MediaTek MT7663 Wi-Fi on an internal USB bus, no Ethernet.
  - It boots **Ubuntu 26.10 (kernel 6.18.59) from USB** with "locked bootloader, no root, no unlock".
  - It cold-boots straight into Ubuntu, reaching SSH in about 43 s.
  - The Android eMMC is backed up byte for byte and left intact.
  - It runs Claude Code natively as an always-on home server.
- **Limits:**
  - No HDMI audio on S905X4 with this kernel.
  - The Wi-Fi chip has a firmware bug that the author says is now "contained" with a power-GPIO fix and a watchdog.
  - An overnight stability run is still pending.
  - The README does not say exactly how a stock locked u-boot is persuaded to boot the USB image. It mentions a `cmd_boot_org` fallback and a custom DTB. **Treat this as a single-developer proof of concept.**
- **Score:** 6/10 · **Confidence:** low (one developer, one unit)

### Inkwell: Alpine Linux and an offline LLM on a Kindle Paperwhite 3/4 (created 2026-10-17; pushed 2026-11-19)
- **What it is:** [pragyaangaur/Inkwell](https://github.com/pragyaangaur/Inkwell) (37★) keeps Amazon's bootloader and kernel and replaces everything after them with an Alpine userspace. It was featured by [Hackaday on 2026-11-06](https://hackaday.com/2026/11/06/its-alpine-linux-on-a-kindle-paperwhite-3/) (page blocked; date and title from the README).
  - It includes a reader (MuPDF, panel-by-panel comics), Doom, Minecraft rd-132211, chess, a terminal and SSH.
  - It also includes "Quill", which runs **SmolLM2 through llama.cpp on the Kindle's own CPU** at about 1.7 words/s, fully offline.
- **Needs:** an already jailbroken PW3 (7th gen) or PW4 (10th gen).
- **Jailbreak status:** [KindleModding/SpiderCat](https://github.com/KindleModding/SpiderCat) (source repo created 2026-10-01) builds a book that exploits a WebKit `JSArray::sort` use-after-free in the webreader. It supports firmware **5.16.3–5.19.5**, and per its README **5.19.6 and later patched it**. Search summaries say a newer method, Véra, was still unpatched on 5.19.6 ([the-ebook-reader, 2026-08-11](https://blog.the-ebook-reader.com/2026/08/11/new-vera-jailbreak-released-for-latest-kindles-with-current-software/), blocked).
- **Advice:** keep Kindles you want to mod offline.
- **Score:** 6/10 · **Confidence:** medium

### fit3-toolkit: native apps and firmware patches for the Samsung Galaxy Fit3, reverse-engineered with Claude Code (created 2026-11-01; pushed 2026-11-22)
- **What it is:** [yksen/fit3-toolkit](https://github.com/yksen/fit3-toolkit) (18★). The Fit3 (SM-R390) "has no SDK and no way to install apps".
  - The toolkit builds a firmware package from *your own* stock image and flashes it over Bluetooth RFCOMM.
  - It adds an "Application Hub" of native-looking LVGL apps (`.f3x`) that install in about a second without reflashing.
  - It also patches in a reply keyboard for notifications, and ships an emulator that runs the real firmware's functions on a PC.
- **AI angle (README):** "developed with Claude Code (Anthropic): writing code, debugging, reverse-engineering the firmware, tests and documentation."
- **Limits:** tested on one watch, firmware `R390XXU0AZA3` only, and the build refuses any other version. Flashing has been tried from Linux only.
- **Score:** 6/10 · **Confidence:** low

### Yandex Station Lite: root without soldering, and the "glagol" LAN protocol reversed (created 2026-10-24)
- **What it is:** [ilia-ershov/yandex-station-lite-research](https://github.com/ilia-ershov/yandex-station-lite-research), a researcher's write-up on their own device. The speaker runs Buildroot Linux, kernel 4.9.68 aarch64, on an Amlogic quad-core SoC with a TAS5825M amp.
  - Root came from the firmware still trusting CA certificates whose private keys had leaked. The device was coaxed into starting a root ADB server on the researcher's own LAN.
  - The researcher then reversed the single 21 MB `maind` binary and the local "glagol" protocol, far enough to make the assistant speak arbitrary text.
- **Relevance:** the speaker is mainly sold in Russia. The pattern is the transferable part: an old trust store becomes a root path on a cheap Linux speaker.
- **Score:** 5/10 · **Confidence:** medium

## 3. AI-assisted hardware hacking

- **CleanYourTV, cleantv and tellyscope (Android TV):** the agent is the end-user installer, guided by a repo of per-model package lists and safety scripts. What still needs humans: the curated lists (200+ contributors), and the judgment about which packages a given TV needs to boot. Tellyscope hard-codes a protected list "with no override".
- **fit3-toolkit (Samsung Galaxy Fit3):** Claude Code was used for firmware reverse engineering, the LVGL app framework, the emulator and the docs. Manual work: owning a real watch on one exact firmware, Bluetooth flashing tests, and keeping Samsung's code out of the repo.
- **photo-frame-dashboard:** written to be driven by an agent. Its rules for agents include "Don't trust `OKAY`": fastboot reported a successful write on the Skylight that never landed. Writes and RAM payloads "need explicit approval for that specific action". This is a useful model for agent safety on hardware.
- **xiaomibox-ubuntu:** a TV box turned into a Claude Code host, with Claude also used in its bring-up.
- **[yzddmr6/android-mainline-port](https://github.com/yzddmr6/android-mainline-port)** (126★, created 2026-10-20): an agent skill (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI) distilled from three Qualcomm tablet ports.
  - The tablets: Xiaomi Pad 6 Pro (SM8475), OnePlus Pad 2 Pro and Xiaomi Pad 8 Pro (SM8750).
  - It covers mainline kernels 6.17 to 7.4 and Ubuntu 26.04 + GNOME.
  - It encodes 40+ decision points, for example to check device-tree msm-id/board-id when `fastboot boot` returns OKAY but the device hangs.
- **[petersameh/tc001-unlocked](https://github.com/petersameh/tc001-unlocked)** (created 2026-11-04): replacement firmware for the Ulanzi TC001 ESP32 pixel clock, "built step by step with Claude Code", with the prompts published. It is small (0★), but it is a reproducible recipe.
- **[LiamZJU/kindle-weread-skill](https://github.com/LiamZJU/kindle-weread-skill)** (16★, created 2026-10-22): an agent skill that jailbreaks a Kindle, installs KOReader and deploys WeRead plugins. The full jailbreak-to-apps pipeline is now packaged for agents.
- **[weasel79/fm1-echo-show](https://github.com/weasel79/fm1-echo-show)** (created 2026-11-21): ports the FM-1 synth emulator (sector G, 2026-11-22) to a Linux Echo Show 8 2021. Two of this week's revivals are already being combined.
- **Policy friction (secondary only):** a [ToolBit article](https://www.toolbit.ai/blog/claude-codex-reverse-engineer-own-device) (blocked; search summary) reports refusals and account restrictions for users analysing their own devices. It cites 404 Media on 2026-11-10. **Not verified.**

## 5. Hidden opportunities

1. **Echo Show 8 2nd gen as a local-vision node** (hypothesis)
   - **Hardware:** an MT8183 with an open mainline kernel and a 13 MP camera already working under TECHO5.
   - **Opportunity:** MT8183 is a Chromebook-class SoC that, as far as this analyst recalls (not verified today), includes an "APU" AI accelerator. No one in these repos uses it.
   - **Why overlooked:** the Echo work has focused on voice.
   - **What could be built:** a privacy-local doorbell or kitchen camera that runs a small vision model.
   - **Validation experiment:** on an athena unit running TECHO5, check whether any mainline or ChromeOS APU driver exists. Failing that, benchmark a TFLite model on the CPU's 4×A73 cores.
2. **Photo-frame vendor apps as a home-network security audit target**
   - **Hardware:** cheap A33/RK3126 frames, sold under dozens of brand names.
   - **Opportunity:** the AiMOR finding shows that at least one firmware gives its vendor server a root shell.
   - **Why overlooked:** nobody audits $50 frames.
   - **What could be built:** a containment checklist and firewall profile, or a "safe firmware" replacement.
   - **Validation experiment:** with an owned frame on an isolated VLAN, capture the traffic and see whether other brands' apps run a similar command channel.
3. **Locked-bootloader Amlogic S905X4 boxes as Linux servers** (speculative)
   - **Hardware:** the xiaomibox-ubuntu result suggests the stock u-boot environment can boot a USB image without unlocking.
   - **Opportunity:** if this generalises to other S905X4 boxes, a large pool of cheap boxes in India becomes low-power Linux servers.
   - **Validation experiment:** **unverified on any other model.** Dump `printenv` from a second S905X4 box, read-only, and compare boot commands.
4. **Skylight / RK3566 wall calendars**
   - **Opportunity:** some units ship as userdebug builds with root, and TECHO5 runs mainline Linux 6.18 on one.
   - **What could be built:** with a 15.6" 1080p panel, this is a dashboard that needs no tablet hack.
   - **Validation experiment:** read the firmware string in Settings → About before buying used, and target D156 builds.

## 8. Long-term opportunity radar (6–24 months)

- **Agents as installers for consumer devices:** CleanYourTV's growth (about 60k★ in five weeks) suggests more "repo + prompt" packages for TVs, frames, wearables and e-readers. Expect vendors to respond, either by restricting ADB on TV firmware or by removing `pm disable-user` routes. That is speculative, and worth watching in TV firmware changelogs.
- **Amazon Echo end of road:** every Echo made through 2021 is now open; nothing made after 2021 is. As Alexa+ and Vega OS push older units out of support, used 2019–2021 Echo Shows become the cheapest open smart displays.
- **Mainline Linux on MT8163/MT8183:** two independent kernel efforts (TECHO5 6.18 and linux-amazon-echo 7.2.13) plus bengris32's MT8163 work make the camera, audio and display drivers reusable for other MT8163/MT8183 devices (Fire tablets, Chromebooks).
- **Kindle cat-and-mouse:** each jailbreak (WinterBreak → AdBreak → SpiderCat → Véra) gets patched within months, and 5.19.6 closed SpiderCat. Buy used Kindles still on old firmware, and keep them offline.
- **Qualcomm tablets on mainline:** agent skills that encode porting knowledge (android-mainline-port) and flagship ports (TB323FU) suggest 2026–27 Snapdragon tablets may become credible ARM Linux machines within a year of release.
